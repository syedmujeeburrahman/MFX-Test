1. Executive Summary

Fox Cities Racquet Club requires an Odoo-based operating system to manage indoor

court reservations, player/member handling, club memberships, court billing, retail

inventory, point-of-sale operations, purchase replenishment, sales, accounting, and

customer pipeline activities.


The core custom module prepared for this business is FCRC Court Seat Booking . It

provides a booking dashboard, court configuration, seat/time availability, membership-

aware billing, non-member entry fee logic, ball machine rental billing, booking lifecycle

tracking, reminders, no-show handling, membership invoicing, and booking analysis.


This BRD extends that custom module into a wider implementation scope covering:


    Accounting

    Sales

    Purchase

    Point of Sale

    Barcode
    Inventory

    CRM

    Products and retail stock

    Membership and court booking operations




2. Business Objectives

The implementation should help the club:


    Manage court availability by date, time, court, and seat capacity.

    Support both person/hour and court/hour billing policies.

    Track members and non-members clearly from the contact record.

    Invoice court bookings, memberships, non-member fees, and ball machine

    rentals.

    Prevent invalid booking operations such as past booking, early court start, double

    booking, and over-capacity booking.

    Track operational exceptions such as potential no-shows, late starts, and courts

    not ended on time.

    Maintain demo-ready stock for racquets, strings, balls, hats, and accessories.

    Sell club retail products through Sales and POS.

    Purchase and replenish retail inventory.

    Use barcode scanning for product lookup and inventory/POS workflows.

    Track prospects for memberships, lessons, events, and corporate/group

    opportunities in CRM.

    Provide accounting visibility for invoices, payments, revenue, and receivables.

4. Court Booking Requirements

4.1 Court Configuration

Each court must support:


    Court name and code.

    Court type, such as indoor or outdoor.

    Player seat capacity.

    Billing basis:

        Person / Hour

        Court / Hour


    Hourly rate.

    Active/inactive status.

    Internal notes.


Business rule:


    If billing basis is Person / Hour , booking availability is based on the number of

    available player seats.

    If billing basis is Court / Hour , any booking blocks the whole court, even if only

    two players are entered.



4.2 Dashboard Booking Experience
The dashboard should provide:


    Date selection using Today, Tomorrow, and custom date picker.

    Preview time and duration selection.

    Court cards with seat availability.

    Fixed-size seat cards for consistent layout.

    A View Times action to show available and booked hours for the selected

    court/day.

    Time slot cards showing available, full, partial, or past status.

    X close control for the time slot panel.

    Bookable future slots only.

    Court-level status such as available, partial, full, and in use.



4.3 Booking Creation

Users should be able to:


    Select a court.

    Select an available time slot.

    Select one or more players.

    Add ball machine rental if required.

    Add booking notes.


System validations:


    Past date/time bookings are not allowed.

    End time must be after start time.

    Selected players must not exceed court capacity.

    Court/hour bookings block the entire court.

    Person/hour bookings only consume selected player seats.
    Existing overlapping bookings must be considered before confirming availability.



4.4 Booking Lifecycle

Booking states:


    Draft

    Reserved

    In Use

    Completed

    Invoiced

    No Show

    Cancelled


Expected flow:


 1. Booking is created in Draft or Reserved.

 2. Reserved booking can be started only within allowed time window.

 3. Start Play records actual start time and moves booking to In Use.

 4. End Play records actual stop time and moves booking to Completed.

 5. Create Invoice creates a draft customer invoice and moves booking to Invoiced.

 6. Cancelled and No Show states remain available for exception handling.



4.5 Start and End Play Rules

Start Play:


    Only reserved bookings can be started.

    Booking cannot start after scheduled end time.

    Players may start only within 15 minutes before scheduled start time.
     If the player arrives earlier than the 15-minute buffer, the system must show a

     validation error.

     Start Play records actual start time for audit purposes.


End Play:


     Only in-use bookings can be ended.

     Actual stop time must be after actual start time.

     End Play records actual stop time.

     Actual play time is for internal audit only and must not change billing.


Billing rule:


     Billing is based on scheduled booking hours, not consumed or actual play hours.



4.6 No-Show and Operational Attention

The system should identify:


     Reserved bookings where start time has passed.

     Reserved bookings where end time has passed.

     In-use bookings where scheduled end time has passed but play was not ended.

     Bookings starting within the next 30 minutes.


Operational indicators:


     Potential No Show

     Past Start

     Starts Soon

     End Overdue


Automated reminders:
    Activity reminder for bookings starting within the next 30 minutes.

    Activity reminder for in-use bookings not ended after scheduled end time.



4.7 Cancellation

Cancellation should:


    Require a cancellation reason.

    Store cancelled by user.

    Store cancellation date/time.

    Not allow cancellation where a linked invoice exists unless the draft invoice is

    deleted first.



4.8 Booking Invoicing

Invoice should include:


    Court/person or court/hour charge based on court billing policy.

    Non-member entry fee where applicable.

    Ball machine rental line where selected.


Invoice behavior:


    Draft invoice is linked to booking.

    Invoice smart button opens the linked invoice.

    If draft invoice is deleted, booking invoice link should clear and operational

    buttons should become available again.

    Cancelled and no-show bookings should not be invoiceable.




5. Membership Requirements
5.1 Membership Plan

The club should be able to create named membership plans with:


    Plan name.

    Description.

    Duration days.

    Price.

    Proration option.

    Active/inactive status.


Business decision:


    No separate family/adult membership type is required for the demo.

    Membership identification is handled through the plan name.

    Family eligibility is handled operationally outside ERP for this demo.



5.2 Membership Product Handling

Membership plans require invoice products only for accounting invoice lines.


System behavior:


    The system should create and maintain the linked service product automatically.

    Product should be configured as service.

    Product should be saleable.

    Product should not be purchaseable.

    Customer taxes should be empty unless the business later decides otherwise.

    Product form should not be part of the membership creation workflow.



5.3 Membership Year
The system should support:


    Membership year name.

    Start and end dates.

    Non-member booking limit per membership year.


Default period:


    September 1 to August 31.



5.4 Membership Sale / Invoice

Users should be able to invoice membership directly from:


    Contact form.

    Members list.

    Non-members list.


The wizard should:


    Select contact.

    Select membership plan.

    Select membership year.

    Compute start date, end date, duration, annual price, and prorated price.

    Create a draft customer invoice.

    Create the membership line linked to the invoice.



5.5 Membership Status

A contact can have only one active membership covering the same period.


Membership statuses:


    Active Member
    Expired Member

    Non-member


Menus:


    Members: contacts with active membership.

    Non-members: contacts without active membership.


Booking usage:


    Member/non-member status is used during booking pricing.

    Non-members may be charged entry fees.

    Non-member booking limits may apply within a membership year.

17. Assumptions

    The club will provide final court list, product list, pricing, vendors, and accounting

    mappings.

    Membership naming will be enough for staff to identify member type/benefits.
    Family member handling will remain outside ERP for the demo.

    Billing is based on scheduled booking duration.

    Actual play time is stored for internal audit only.

    Court/hour bookings reserve the whole court.

    Person/hour bookings reserve only selected player seats.

    POS will initially be used for retail products.

    Court and membership invoicing will remain in their respective workflows for the

    demo.




18. Acceptance Criteria

The implementation is acceptable when:


    Users can book courts through the dashboard without overbooking.

    Users can clearly see available and booked time slots.

    Court/hour policy blocks the whole court.

    Person/hour policy allows seat-level booking.

    Invalid past bookings are blocked with clear validation.

    Start Play respects the 15-minute early-arrival buffer.

    End Play records actual stop time.

    No-show and overdue states are visible operationally.

    Booking invoices are accurate and linked to bookings.

    Membership plans can be created without manually opening product forms.

    Membership invoices can be created from contact screens.

    Contacts correctly appear under Members or Non-members.

    Retail products have cost, sale price, and inventory quantities.

    POS can sell stocked retail products.
   Purchase can replenish retail stock.

   Accounting can manage invoices, payments, and vendor bills.

   CRM can track new club opportunities.

   Booking Analysis report opens as pivot/graph/list and shows meaningful

   measures.
